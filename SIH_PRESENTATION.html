🌧️ Monsoon & Seasonality Intelligence

Our model captures India's critical maritime seasons:

🌊 Monsoon (Jun–Sep)

Freight Rate Premium: +15–20%

  • ✗ Higher sea volatility → higher insurance
  • ✗ Reduced port capacity
  • ✗ Weather delays & repositioning costs
  • Strategy: Pre-book before June or defer to October

❄️ Winter (Nov–Feb)

Freight Rate Lows: 5–10% discount

  • ✓ Calm seas → faster transit
  • ✓ Higher vessel availability
  • ✓ Competitive shipping rates
  • Strategy: Maximize winter procurement
