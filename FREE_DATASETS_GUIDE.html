📈 FREIGHT RATES DATA

✅ BEST OPTION: Baltic Exchange via FRED

  • Source: Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)
  • URL: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/
  • Data: Baltic Dry Index (BDI), Capesize, Panamax rates
  • Coverage: Daily, 2000-2026
  • Cost: FREE

FRED Datasets You Need:

  • BCI - Capesize Index (daily) → https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BCI
  • BPI - Panamax Index (daily)
  • BHSI - Handymax Index (daily)
  • BDINUSD - Baltic Dry Index (daily)

⛽ COMMODITY PRICES

✅ BEST OPTION: World Bank Commodity Data

  • Source: World Bank Pink Sheet
  • URL: https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets
  • Data: Coal & Iron Ore prices (monthly)
  • Coverage: 1960-2026
  • Cost: FREE

Sample Data (Coal Prices):

2020: $48.50/ton 2021: $85.20/ton (↑ 76%) 2022: $150.80/ton (↑ 77% - war impact) 2026: $115.20/ton (today)

Download Method:

  1. Visit: https://data.worldbank.org/topic/11
  2. Search: "Coal Price" or "Iron Ore Price"
  3. Download: CSV file

🏭 PORT CAPACITY DATA

✅ BEST OPTION: Indian Ports Association (IPA)

  • Source: IPA Official Portal
  • URL: https://ipa.nic.in/
  • Data: Port-wise cargo, capacity, utilization
  • Coverage: All Indian Major Ports
  • Cost: FREE

Available Metrics:

  • Port-wise cargo handled (MMT/month)
  • Capacity utilization percentage
  • Average turnaround time
  • Berth occupancy ratio
  • Vessel waiting time

Download Steps:

  1. Visit https://ipa.nic.in/
  2. Navigate: "Monthly Performance Reviews"
  3. Download: Latest Excel file
  4. Extract: Vizag & Paradip data

🌧️ WEATHER DATA

✅ BEST OPTION: IMD (Indian Meteorological Department)

  • Source: India Meteorological Department
  • URL: https://www.imdpune.gov.in/
  • Data: Rainfall, wind speed, cyclone forecasts
  • Coverage: NER & coastal India (1960-2026)
  • Cost: FREE

Download Method:

  1. Visit https://www.imdpune.gov.in/Welcomefiles/
  2. Search: "Climatological Data"
  3. Download: Monthly rainfall data
  4. Filter: NER regions
